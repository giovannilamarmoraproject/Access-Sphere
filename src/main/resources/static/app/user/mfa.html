🔐 Configure Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) adds an extra layer of protection to your account by requiring not just your password, but also an additional verification step—such as a code from your phone or email. This significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password gets compromised.

By enabling MFA, you’re taking a proactive step toward securing your personal data, protecting sensitive company resources, and complying with modern security best practices. Choose one or more verification methods below to get started.
